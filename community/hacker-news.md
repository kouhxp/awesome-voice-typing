## Method and sources

- Discovery: [HN Search, powered by Algolia](https://hn.algolia.com/) and its [search API](https://hn.algolia.com/api). Searches covered story titles, submitted URLs, and submission text; candidate results were checked against the README’s project identities.
- Verification: [official Hacker News API](https://github.com/HackerNews/API). Titles, points (`score`), total comments (`descendants`, including replies), and submission dates (`time`) were checked using each story’s item endpoint, for example [Whispering’s launch record](https://hacker-news.firebaseio.com/v0/item/44942731.json).
- Scope: launches, releases, technical write-ups, benchmarks, and integrations substantially about a directory tool. Incidental mentions, unrelated namesakes, and related projects were excluded. Each HN submission appears once; separate qualifying submissions about the same project are retained.
- Identity checks include Epicenter Whispering’s former `braden-w` / `epicenter-so` names, OpenLess’s former `appergb` owner, Speak to AI’s Dabri name, and Muesli’s `freedspeech.xyz` domain. The Handy mouse integration uses Handy. Yap refers specifically to `FrigadeHQ/yap`, and OpenWhispr to `OpenWhispr/openwhispr`.
